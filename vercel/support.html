Common troubleshooting steps and frequently asked questions.

Troubleshooting

Whenever Yapper updates itself, it wipes Voidcord. You might see a notification in Yapper telling you that you need to update Voidcord, or it might just disappear from your settings completely. All you have to do is re-run VoidcordInstaller.exe from your installer folder and hit Install again. None of your themes or settings will be lost.

Yapper is probably still running in the background. Close Yapper completely (check Task Manager if it won't close) and run the installer again. If it still gives you an error, right-click the installer and choose "Run as administrator".

Check your internet connection or see if GitHub is down. If the installer can't download the update on its own, you can just grab voidcord.js manually and drop it straight into your yapper-desktop resources folder.

Honestly, it might just be your themes. A lot of custom themes use heavy blurs, animations, or messy CSS that tanks your FPS and makes Yapper feel sluggish. Try turning off your themes one by one to see which one is causing it, or clear out your Quick CSS if you have a bunch of snippets saved.

A broken theme is probably crashing the app. Head over to your yapper-desktop folder, open voidcord/themes, and delete or rename the theme file you just added. If you know how to use DevTools (Ctrl+Shift+I), you can also type localStorage.removeItem('voidcord_enabled_themes') to turn off all themes instantly.
